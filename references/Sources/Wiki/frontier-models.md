# Frontier Models

**Summary**: The most capable AI systems at any given time -- a moving target that defines what is possible and what is about to be disrupted.

**Sources**: Early science acceleration experiments with GPT-5.pdf; Chakrabarty et al. - 2026 - Readers Prefer Outputs of AI Trained on Copyrighted Books over Expert Human Writers.pdf

**Last updated**: 2026-05-11

---

## What defines the frontier

Both sources use "frontier" to mean the most capable commercially available models at the time of study. In [[chakrabarty-et-al-2026]], the frontier consisted of GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Gemini 1.5 Pro (closed-source models accessible without technical expertise). Open-weights Llama 3.1 was tested but performed poorly at following long-context instructions. (source: Chakrabarty et al. 2026)

In [[bubeck-et-al-2025]], the frontier is [[gpt-5]] and specifically GPT-5 Pro, with references to internal models that can "think for a few hours" and achieve results GPT-5 Pro cannot. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)

## Frontier capabilities circa 2025-2026

As documented across both sources, frontier models can:

- Solve Olympiad-level geometry problems (source: Chakrabarty et al. 2026)
- Achieve a rating of 2700 on Codeforces (source: Chakrabarty et al. 2026)
- Deliver medical guidance meeting professional standards (source: Chakrabarty et al. 2026)
- Produce creative writing preferred over expert human writing when fine-tuned (source: Chakrabarty et al. 2026)
- Contribute verified novel mathematical proofs (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- Derive analytical results in physics beyond symbolic computation software (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- Perform cross-disciplinary literature search by concept rather than keyword (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)

## The rate of change

A recurring theme in [[bubeck-et-al-2025]] is the speed of progress: "None of this would have been possible just twelve months ago." The paper also notes that internal models already exceed GPT-5 Pro on some tasks, suggesting the public frontier lags behind the internal one. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)

This rate matters for the book's arc: the capabilities documented in these papers are snapshots of a rapidly moving target.
