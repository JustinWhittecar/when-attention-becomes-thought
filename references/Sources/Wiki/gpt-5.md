# GPT-5

**Summary**: OpenAI's frontier language model as of late 2025, the first general-purpose AI system documented to contribute verified novel scientific results across multiple disciplines.

**Sources**: Early science acceleration experiments with GPT-5.pdf

**Last updated**: 2026-05-11

---

## Capabilities as documented

The [[bubeck-et-al-2025]] paper provides the most detailed public account of GPT-5's scientific capabilities. Key demonstrated abilities:

- **Extended reasoning**: GPT-5 Pro can reason for sustained periods (5-40 minutes in documented cases, with internal models capable of "thinking for a few hours"). The convex optimization warm-up took 17 minutes; the black hole symmetry derivation took 18 minutes; the cosmic string integral took 40 minutes. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- **Symbolic mathematics**: Produced verified proofs including improving a convex optimization bound, deriving Lie point symmetries, solving analytic integrals beyond Mathematica's reach, and contributing the key step to solving Erdos Problem #848. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- **Code generation**: Built a complete PDE solver for thermonuclear burn propagation including discretization, operator splitting, and optimization -- "in minutes" what would have taken a domain expert "a few work days." (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- **Cross-disciplinary literature search**: Identified connections across mathematical subfields and scientific disciplines by focusing on conceptual rather than terminological similarity. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)

## Limitations as documented

The authors are unusually frank:

- "Confidently makes mistakes, ardently defends them, and can confuse itself (and us) in the process." (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- Results depend on prompt details and are "challenging to reproduce." (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- In numerical work, introduces silent simplifications and declares false victories. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- Lacks perception of mathematical "negative space" -- doesn't realize when obvious counterexamples block a strategy. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
- Overconfident in existing methods; doesn't systematically reason about why certain approaches are inherently unable to solve certain problems. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)

## GPT-5 in the historical arc

GPT-5 represents a specific point on the trajectory from early neural networks to systems that participate in scientific discovery. The paper frames this carefully: "These contributions are modest in scope but profound in implication, given the rate at which frontier AI is progressing." The authors position GPT-5 as the beginning of a trend, not its culmination. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)

The paper is from November 2025. The fact that internal models could already derive optimal bounds "from scratch" -- results GPT-5 Pro could only approximate -- suggests the capability frontier was advancing even during the writing. (source: Bubeck et al. 2025)
